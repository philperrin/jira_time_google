Submit Jira worklogs based on your calendar events!

This web app syncs pulls your Jira tasks in and allows you to schedule time on your calendar based on those tasks. You can then pull your weekly calendar events in to the app and generate Jira worklogs based on your calendar.

Philosophy

The idea behind this app is based on a few guiding principles:

  • If you have work to do, it needs to be captured in a Jira task.
  • Work can only be performed if it is scheduled.
  • Your calendar is the source of truth for scheduled work.
